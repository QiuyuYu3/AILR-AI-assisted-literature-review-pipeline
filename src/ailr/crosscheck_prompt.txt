# VERDICTS

For every field, return one verdict, a one-sentence reason, and a confidence from 1 to 10.

- agree — the quote supports the recorded value and nothing in the paper contradicts it.
- disagree — the quote does not support the value, the paper states something different, or the
  value does not answer what the field definition asks for.
- uncertain — you cannot locate the passage that would settle it either way.

Use uncertain sparingly. It means the evidence is not findable, not that you dislike the value:
a record you can check and find sound is agree, and one the paper contradicts is disagree.

# RULES

- Judge the record as given. Do not re-extract the paper, and do not mark disagree merely because
  you would have worded the value differently or chosen a different supporting quote.
- A defensible value with a badly chosen quote is still agree; note the weak quote in your reason.
- "Not reported" is a legitimate value when the paper does not report the item.
- When you claim the paper contradicts the value, quote the passage that shows it.
- Keep each reason to one sentence.
- Fill suggested_value only when you mark disagree and the paper states a specific different
  value. Leave it null otherwise.
